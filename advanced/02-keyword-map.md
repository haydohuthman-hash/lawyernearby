## Deliberately not targeting

- **Interstate / national removalist terms** — outside our real service area and
  positioning; chasing this traffic wastes content effort and doesn't convert.
- **Backloading / shared-truck terms** — not our business model; don't build pages
  that promise something we don't deliver.
- **Generic "removalists near me" as a page** — this is what Google Business Profile
  + the location pages already own; a separate page would just compete with our own
  `/moving-brisbane` and `/moving-gold-coast`.
