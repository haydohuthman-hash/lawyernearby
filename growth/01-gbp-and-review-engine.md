# Residence Relocations — GBP + Review Engine Playbook

**Goal:** fastest possible qualified-lead flow. Google Business Profile (GBP) ranks
in the Map Pack far quicker than the website ranks in organic, and reviews lift
*both* Map Pack rank and paid-ad conversion. This is the #1 quick win — do it first.

**Owner:** whoever holds the Google account for the business.
**Time to implement:** ~2 hours setup, then 10 min/week.

---

      `...?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=profile`

## PART 2 — The Review Engine

Reviews are the highest-ROI compounding asset you have. Target: **4–8 new reviews/month**,
which within ~90 days makes you competitive in the Map Pack and lifts every ad's conversion rate.

### The mechanics
1. **Timing is everything** — send the ask at *peak delight*: truck has left, nothing broke,
   customer is standing in their new place. Not 3 days later.
2. **One tap** — send a **direct review link** (get it from GBP → "Ask for reviews" → copy link,
   or `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID`). Never make them search.
3. **Two touches max** — SMS at move completion, email follow-up next morning if no review.
4. **Make it a habit** — the crew lead sends the SMS from a work phone before leaving site. Systemise it.

### SMS script (send on-site, crew lead's phone)
> Hi {FirstName}, it's {CrewLead} from Residence Relocations 👋 Thanks for trusting us with
> your move today. If everything went well, a 30-second Google review means the world to a
> local team like ours — here's the direct link: {REVIEW_LINK}
> Any issues at all, reply here first and I'll sort it.

*(That last line is the safety valve — unhappy customers reply to you privately instead of
leaving a public 1-star.)*

### Email follow-up (next morning, only if no review yet)
**Subject:** How did we do, {FirstName}?

> Hi {FirstName},
>
> We hope you're settling into {NewSuburb}. It was a pleasure moving you {FromSuburb} → {NewSuburb} yesterday.
>
> We're a small, local team and Google reviews are genuinely how families here find us. If we
> earned it, would you leave a quick line about your experience? It takes about 30 seconds:
>
> 👉 {REVIEW_LINK}
>
> If anything wasn't perfect, just reply to this email — I read every one personally.
>
> Thanks,
> {Name}, Residence Relocations

### Review-response templates (respond to EVERY review within 24h — it's a ranking signal)

**5-star response** (work the keyword in naturally):
> Thank you {FirstName}! It was a pleasure handling your {FromSuburb} to {ToSuburb} move. Fixed price,
> no surprises — exactly how a Brisbane to Gold Coast move should go. Enjoy the new place! 🏡

**4-star response:**
> Thanks {FirstName}, really appreciate the feedback and the 4 stars. We'd love to know what would've
> made it a 5 — always looking to tighten up. Enjoy {ToSuburb}!

**Negative review response** (calm, specific, offline the resolution):
> {FirstName}, thank you for the honest feedback and I'm sorry {specific issue} fell short of the
> standard we hold ourselves to. That's not the Residence Relocations experience we promise. I'd like
> to make it right — I've sent you a direct message / please call me on {phone}. — {Owner name}

### Where else to seed reviews (diversify beyond Google)
- [ ] **Product Review (productreview.com.au)** — the AU trust signal customers check for movers.
- [ ] **Facebook page recommendations** — feeds the Meta ad social-proof loop.
- [ ] **Word of Mouth (WOMO)** if still active in your area.
- Keep Google as the priority; ~70% of asks point to Google, rotate the rest.

## PART 3 — The referral-channel reinforcement (don't skip)

Your agents/stagers/designers are a review + citation goldmine, and this is where paid + referral
compound instead of competing:

- [ ] Ask each **partner agent** for a Google review from their business account
      ("Trusted removalist we refer clients to") — B2B reviews carry weight and read as authority.
- [ ] Give partners the **direct review link** to forward to *their* clients post-move.
- [ ] Every partner referral should still get the standard review SMS — those customers are your
      warmest reviewers.
- [ ] ⚠️ **Tension to avoid:** never run GBP Posts or ads that undercut a partner's positioning
      (e.g. "cut out the middleman"). Partners are a channel, not a competitor — the messaging to
      their audience is "your agent trusts us", never "skip your agent".
