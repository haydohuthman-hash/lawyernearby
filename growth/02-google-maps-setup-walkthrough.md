## PART 1 — Create / claim the listing

1. Go to **google.com/business** (or search your business name in Google Maps → "Add your business").
2. Sign in with the Google account that should **own** this forever (use a business Gmail, not a
   personal one you might lose access to — e.g. `hello@residencerelocations.com.au` on Google Workspace).
3. Business name: **Residence Relocations** — type it *exactly*, no keywords appended
   (adding "Brisbane Gold Coast Removalists" to the name risks suspension).
4. Business category (primary): **Mover** (see Part 3 for the full category set to add after).
5. When asked "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?":
   - **No** if you're home-based or don't want a public shopfront → this makes you a
     **Service-Area Business (SAB)**. Recommended for a corridor mover.
   - **Yes** only if you have a real staffed commercial address.
6. Service areas: add both ends of the corridor (full list in Part 3).
7. Add phone + website (use the UTM website link in Part 3 so GBP traffic is trackable).
8. Finish → you'll be pushed into **verification**.

## PART 2 — Verification (the step that trips people up)

Google decides which methods you get. For a **service-area / mover** business in Australia,
expect one or more of:

- **Video verification** (most common now for SABs) — you record a single unbroken video showing:
  (a) your location/service area, (b) proof you're a real operating business (branded truck,
  equipment, tools, signage), and (c) proof you manage the business (log into the dashboard on
  camera). **Have a branded truck / uniform / equipment ready to film** — this is the fastest path.
- **Phone/SMS** — a code to your business number (instant, but not always offered to SABs).
- **Postcard** — a code mailed to your address (~5 business days; only if you listed an address).

**Tips to pass video verification first go:**
- Film in one continuous take, no cuts.
- Show the truck with "Residence Relocations" branding + a moving-blanket/dolly/trolley.
- Show a piece of mail or a document with the business name + address.
- End by logging into the Business Profile on your phone on camera.
- Don't stage a fake location — mismatches trigger suspension.

Until verified, you can build out the profile but it **won't show publicly**.

## Pitfalls that get listings suspended (avoid all)
- ❌ Keywords in the business name ("...Brisbane Gold Coast Cheap Removalists").
- ❌ A fake/virtual/PO-box address, or an address you don't actually operate from.
- ❌ Multiple listings for the same business (creates duplicates — one profile only).
- ❌ Review gating or buying reviews (ask everyone, never filter to only 5-stars via a paywall).
- ❌ Mismatched NAP between GBP, website and directories.

## Referral-channel note
Once live, ask your **partner agents** to leave a Google review from their business account
("a removalist we trust with our clients") — B2B reviews read as authority and are a legitimate,
high-value first set of reviews for a new profile. Never message a partner's own audience in a way
that undercuts them; the listing reinforces the referral relationship, it doesn't compete with it.
