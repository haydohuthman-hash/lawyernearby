## Notes before sending
- Replace every `[bracketed]` field, especially your name, phone, email and website.
- Personalise the first line with the agency name at minimum; a real person's name lifts reply rates a lot.
- Follow up once after ~4–5 business days if there's no reply.
- These are business contact addresses published by each agency for enquiries. Include an
  unsubscribe/opt-out line and honour it — under the Australian Spam Act 2003, commercial
  emails need a functional opt-out and accurate sender details.
