What a Brisbane to Gold Coast Move Actually Looks Like, Start to Finish

Updated 13 July 2026

This is a general walkthrough of what a typical Brisbane to Gold Coast move involves — not one specific customer's story, just a realistic picture of the process end to end, since "what actually happens on moving day" is one of the most common things people want to know before booking.

2–4 weeks out: the quote

Most bookings start with our quote calculator — home size, addresses, and a move date give an instant fixed-price estimate. A follow-up call or a couple of photos (for anything unusual — see our guide to what a quote should include) locks in the final price and confirms crew size.

The week before: packing

Most self-packed moves box up the non-essentials first — books, decor, out-of-season items — and leave daily essentials until the last day or two. Our box-packing guide and furniture-wrapping guide cover this in detail if you're packing yourself rather than booking a full-service pack.

Moving day morning: the crew arrives

The crew does a quick walkthrough to confirm what's moving, loads heavy furniture first against the truck's cab wall, then boxes, then soft items on top — the exact order covered in our truck-loading guide. A typical 3-bedroom home takes 2–4 hours to load depending on access and stairs.

The drive: Brisbane to the Gold Coast

The corridor run itself is usually 1–1.5 hours depending on where exactly you're starting and finishing and M1 traffic conditions at the time — this is the one part of the day that's largely out of anyone's control, which is why we build a realistic buffer into scheduling rather than booking jobs back-to-back with no margin.

Unloading and setup

Furniture goes straight into the rooms you've indicated (a quick labelling system — see our labelling guide — makes this much faster), beds get reassembled, and heavy items get placed exactly where you want them the first time, since re-shifting a wardrobe after it's already positioned is exactly the kind of thing nobody wants to do twice.

The first night

Most people don't fully unpack on day one — bed made, kitchen essentials out, and the rest follows over the coming days. If kids are part of the move, our guide to moving with kids covers the first-night essentials worth having sorted in advance.

Ready to see your own numbers?

This is the general shape of it — your specific move will vary by home size, access, and distance. Get a fixed quote for the actual numbers on your move.
